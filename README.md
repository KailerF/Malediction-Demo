Malediction is a gothic, turn-based RPG set beneath the island of Vaneclift. Betrayed by your commanding officer and sold to Dr. Erasmus Vane for experimentation, you awaken transformed inside Blackthorn Gaol.

Choose a Vampire, Werewolf, or Necromancer and escape through a prison consumed by revolt. Explore subterranean chambers, uncover the truth behind Vane’s experiments, and face the Unchained—the only other survivor of what made you.

A playable Windows demo featuring class-specific abilities, tactical combat, dice-based attacks, timed dodges, and illustrated story sequences. Currently in development.
